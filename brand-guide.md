# AQX Brand Basics

## Brand name
- Arabic: القرماني إكس
- English: ALQURMANI X
- Short mark: AQX

## Visual direction
Premium, modern, precise and technology-led. Use bright, clean surfaces rather than black or dark full-page backgrounds.

## Core palette
| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| AQX Violet | #6336C9 | Primary brand color |
| Violet Light | #8B5CF6 | Highlights and gradients |
| Violet Deep | #392078 | Contrast and depth |
| White | #FFFFFF | Main surface |
| Silver | #AEBBD0 | Borders and metallic accents |
| Sky | #38BDF8 | Secondary accent |
| Blue | #2563EB | Links and active accents |
| Navy | #172554 | Limited emphasis |

## Usage
- Keep logo proportions; do not stretch or recolor it arbitrarily.
- Prefer white or very light backgrounds.
- Use violet as the dominant accent, with restrained silver and sky-blue details.
- Avoid visually busy backgrounds and maintain clear space around the logo.
